Field Console — 07 × 10, fused

You liked both, and they want to be one site: Generative Field supplies the atmosphere (the living canvas, the enormous serif name) and Live Console supplies the credibility (status bar with a real clock, a truthful activity stream, ⌘K navigation). This is the full homepage, not a fragment — hero, apps, publications, about, footer — so you can judge the system end to end.

The fusion is held together by two rules, below. If this direction is right, this mockup is the build spec: everything here is vanilla JS and ports directly into the Astro site.

Coral — the only color

The italic in your name, hover states, primary buttons, publication years, prompts, and status dots. One hue, so nothing competes; hierarchy comes from scale and weight.

Live is a behavior, not a hue

The earlier green accent clashed with coral, so "live" is now signaled by form instead: the pulsing dot, the blinking caret, the streaming text. Status labels sit in neutral steel with a small coral dot.

One ground, three voices

Instrument Serif speaks as the person, Space Grotesk as the interface, JetBrains Mono as the machine. The particle field runs under the hero only — sections sit on solid ground so reading stays effortless.
