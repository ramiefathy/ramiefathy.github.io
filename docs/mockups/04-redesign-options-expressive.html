Five expressive, interaction-led concepts

Round 1 established systems; this round establishes moments. Each concept below is built around one signature interaction that actually runs in this page — a generative canvas that answers your cursor, a draggable seam between two identities, kinetic editorial type, a working command palette, a horizontally staged portfolio. These are the kinds of committed, single-idea executions that distinguish a memorable personal site from a template.

Everything remains real content and plain language — no themed verbiage. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences, and every palette passes WCAG AA. Each mockup shows the homepage moment plus enough of the work/publication treatment to judge the system; interior pages would inherit the same language at lower intensity.

07Generative Field

A living particle field — hundreds of drifting trails that swirl into a vortex around your cursor — beneath an enormous serif name. The system reads as both organic and computational without a single literal image: exactly your territory.

  • The canvas is the identity: generative, reactive, never the same twice — replaces the old shader hero with something visitors actually play with.
  • Instrument Serif at display scale gives editorial gravity over a technical substrate.
  • Work rows use outlined numerals that ink in on hover; restrained everywhere else so the field stays the star.
  • Reduced motion: the field renders once as a still texture — same atmosphere, no animation.

08Split Identity

The site is literally two sites — a warm serif world for the physician, a dark monospaced world for the builder — divided by a seam the visitor drags. One name set in two typefaces meets at the split. The concept is the biography.

  • The draggable seam is a statement about dual practice, not a gimmick: every piece of content exists in both registers.
  • Below the fold, the two registers fuse into paired panels — clinical description on paper, technical spec on dark.
  • Keyboard accessible (arrow keys move the seam); degrades to a static 50/50 without JS.
  • Endlessly extensible: blog posts, app pages, even the CV can carry the dual reading.

09Kinetic Editorial

Full-screen typographic chapters that snap as you scroll, a ticker strip in constant motion, and work titles that italicize and slide on hover. No images anywhere — the typography is the imagery, at poster scale.

  • Scroll-snap chapters give the homepage cinematic pacing: name → work → publications → contact, each a full frame.
  • One orchestrated load reveal (staggered line rise) — then motion only ever answers the visitor.
  • Hover physics on work rows: title slides and turns italic, ghost numeral inks in.
  • The ticker doubles as a site-wide device — category strips on interior pages.

10Live Console

The site presents itself as a running instrument: a status bar with a live clock, a terminal that streams what you're actually working on, and a real command palette for navigation. For someone whose research is evaluating AI, the medium is the message.

  • The typewriter feed is a truthful activity log, not decoration — it cycles through real current work.
  • The command palette works (open it, type to filter, Esc to close) — power-user navigation as identity.
  • Phosphor green on soot, tempered by Space Grotesk headlines, keeps it refined rather than retro-terminal kitsch.
  • Apps grid reads as a process table: name, one-line purpose, stack — dense and confident.

11Night Gallery

The homepage becomes a darkened room you move through sideways: each project gets its own full-height wall, a giant outlined numeral, and a warm spotlight that follows your cursor. Slow, deliberate, and completely unlike a scrolling feed.

  • Horizontal staging forces one-thing-at-a-time attention — each app is presented, not listed.
  • The cursor spotlight (a warm radial glow) makes the visitor hold the flashlight.
  • Wheel, drag, arrows, and buttons all work; a progress filament tracks the walk.
  • Extends naturally: each wall can later take a live demo or full-bleed capture behind the glow.

Recommendation

08 · Split Identity is the award-shaped one: a single interaction that tells your exact story (physician / builder), infinitely quotable, and no template can imitate it. If you want maximum atmosphere for less structural commitment, 07 · Generative Field delivers the strongest first three seconds and layers cleanly onto a conventional page structure underneath.

These also compose: 07's field could live behind 09's editorial chapters; 10's command palette is worth shipping on any of them as a power-user layer. Pick the concept (or pairing) and I'll build it for real in the Astro site — the mockup interactions here are already vanilla JS, so they port directly.
