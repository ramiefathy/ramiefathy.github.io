REVIEW METHOD AND LIMITS
The expanded review used full-session transcripts with selected source slide
screenshots. It was not a continuous manual listen-through of every recording.
Transcript wording and automatic speech recognition can contain errors;
timestamps, source references, selected figures, and explicit caveats are
included to make key claims checkable against the original material. Selected
screenshots do not represent every slide or every moment of a recording.
Sparse or uncertain transcript spans remain qualified in individual notes.
The 5 poster reviews use the available poster materials. Where a source
was unavailable or a review remains unfinished, that gap stays explicit.

This is a fixed snapshot. Pending items remain pending, and the export does
not fetch updates. The unavailable Valeda recording is not summarized from
its title. Original content, source context, review notes, limitations,
timestamps, and screenshot attributions are preserved byte-for-byte within
the embedded guide-data. Packaging does not change the original private Site.
