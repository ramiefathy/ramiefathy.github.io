Signaling Pathways and Targeted Therapy in Adamantinomatous and Papillary Craniopharyngioma

Abstract

The two craniopharyngioma subtypes are driven by distinct pathways: WNT/β-catenin signaling in adamantinomatous craniopharyngioma (ACP) and BRAF V600E–MAPK signaling in papillary craniopharyngioma (PCP). Step through each mechanism below, then apply a therapy to see its molecular target and which part of the signal it shuts off.
