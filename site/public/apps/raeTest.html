Welcome to the Harm Reduction Companion

This application provides information aimed at reducing potential harms associated with drug use. It is for educational and informational purposes only.

Our Philosophy: We believe in providing objective, fact-based, and non-judgmental information to help individuals make informed decisions. While not using drugs is the safest option, if you choose to use substances, knowing more can help you and others stay safer. This application is guided by the principles of harm reduction, which include respect for the rights of people who use drugs, a commitment to social justice, and the avoidance of stigma.

Critical Disclaimer: Read Carefully

The information provided in this application is for educational and informational purposes only and does not constitute medical or legal advice. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or legal counsel.

Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition, substance use, or treatment options. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read in this application.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number immediately.

The use of any illicit substance carries inherent risks. Information on dosage, effects, and interactions of illicit substances is highly variable due to unknown purity, potency, and potential adulterants. This application does not endorse or encourage the use of illegal substances. Any actions you take based on information from this application are solely at your own risk.

The legal status of substances and related activities can vary significantly by jurisdiction and change over time. It is your sole responsibility to be aware of and comply with all applicable local, state, and federal laws. General Safety & Well-being

EMERGENCY INFORMATION & HELPLINES

If you or someone else is experiencing a life-threatening medical emergency, CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY.

If you are in Baltimore, MD, provide your location clearly to the 911 operator.

IN CASE OF OVERDOSE: CALL 911 FIRST!

Key National Helplines (USA):

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or Text 988. Free, confidential support for people in distress, prevention and crisis resources. Available 24/7.
  • SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-HELP (4357). Free, confidential, 24/7 treatment referral and information service (in English and Spanish) for individuals and families facing mental and/or substance use disorders.
  • Poison Control Center: 1-800-222-1222. For accidental ingestions, exposures to toxins, or drug information in an emergency.
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741. Free, 24/7 crisis support via text message.
  • Never Use Alone Hotline: 1-800-484-3731. A non-judgmental hotline for people who are using drugs alone, to have someone on the line in case of an overdose.

Online Treatment Locators:

  • FindTreatment.gov: SAMHSA online locator for SUD and mental health treatment facilities.

Recognizing an Overdose:

Go to the "Overdose Response" section (main navigation) for detailed signs of overdose by drug class and emergency response steps.

Maryland's Good Samaritan Law: In Maryland, individuals who seek, provide, or assist with medical help for someone experiencing an alcohol or drug-related overdose may be protected from certain criminal charges. (See "Maryland Legal Info" section). Don't let fear of arrest stop you from calling 911 to save a life.

Full Disclaimer

Additional Points:

  • This application is not a substitute for individualized advice from a healthcare professional who knows your medical history.
  • The information about street drugs is inherently limited due to the unregulated nature of their production. Potency, purity, and the presence of adulterants can vary dramatically and unpredictably.
  • While harm reduction strategies aim to reduce negative consequences, they do not eliminate all risks associated with drug use. The safest option is always not to use illicit substances.
  • Information on legal status is for general awareness and is not exhaustive. Laws change frequently. Always consult current local, state, and federal statutes or a legal professional.
