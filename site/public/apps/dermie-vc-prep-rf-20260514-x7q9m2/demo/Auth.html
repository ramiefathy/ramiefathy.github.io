Interim disclosure.

dermie is in active development. A full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are in progress. This page is a narrow, good-faith interim disclosure of what we do and do not do today.

What we collect

Account email (for sign-in), self-reported profile fields (display name, study preferences), and your activity inside the app — question answers, session timing, quiz and case scores, confidence ratings. We operate on Firebase (Google Cloud) and use Gemini (Google) for AI features. Error monitoring may be routed through Sentry.

What we do not do

We do not sell individual user data. We do not make any HIPAA or institutional-grade data handling claims. This product is a board-preparation tool for individual trainees and is not intended for clinical use or patient data.

Deletion

Email hello@dermie.io to request account and data deletion. We will acknowledge within 7 business days while the formal policy is being drafted.

Changes

This interim disclosure will be superseded by a full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before open registration. We will notify users by email when that happens.
