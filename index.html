About Me

I am currently a PGY-4 Dermatology Resident Physician at Johns Hopkins University, with a strong foundation in molecular biology from Princeton University (Summa Cum Laude, Phi Beta Kappa) and medicine from the Perelman School of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania (Alpha Omega Alpha). Originally from Knoxville, Tennessee, I am passionate about integrating clinical care with research and innovation in dermatology. Following this final year of dermatology residency, I am incredibly excited to start a rheumatology-dermatology fellowship program at Mass General-Brigham, where my time will be split between learning from the field's leaders in autoimmune skin disease and conducting major investigations into the applications of generative artificial intelligence as a member of Dr. Adam Rodman's research lab.

My professional journey includes a preliminary year internship at Pennsylvania Hospital, a research fellowship in dermatology at Harvard Medical School, and clinical research at the UCSF Breast Care Center. These experiences have shaped my interests in AI applications in medicine, teledermatology, health equity, and environmental impacts on skin health.

          Dermatology Residency (PGY-4)

          I am currently completing my dermatology residency training (2023-Present) at Johns Hopkins. My preliminary internship year (2022-2023) was completed at Pennsylvania Hospital.

          Clinical Interests

          My interests within dermatology include complex medical dermatology, with particular interests in rheumatology-dermatology and inpatient dermatology. My interests also include teledermatology, applications of generative AI in clinical practice, and exploring integrative approaches to patient care. I am an active member of the Rheumatology-Dermatology Society, Medical Dermatology Society, and Society of Dermatology Hospitalists.
