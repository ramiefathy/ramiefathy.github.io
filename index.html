About Me

I am currently a PGY-3 Dermatology Resident Physician at Johns Hopkins University, with a strong foundation in molecular biology from Princeton University (Summa Cum Laude, Phi Beta Kappa) and medicine from the Perelman School of Medicine at the University of Pennsylvania (Alpha Omega Alpha). Originally from Knoxville, Tennessee, I am passionate about integrating clinical care with research and innovation in dermatology.

My professional journey includes a preliminary year internship at Pennsylvania Hospital, a research fellowship in dermatology at Harvard Medical School, and clinical research at the UCSF Breast Care Center. These experiences have shaped my interests in AI applications in medicine, teledermatology, health equity, and environmental impacts on skin health.

          Clinical Experience & Interests

          Dermatology Residency (PGY-3)

          I am currently completing my dermatology residency training (2023-Present) at Johns Hopkins Hospital, focusing on developing expertise in diagnosing and managing a wide range of skin conditions. My preliminary internship year (2022-2023) was completed at Pennsylvania Hospital.

          Clinical Interests

          My interests within dermatology include medical dermatology, epidemiology, the impact of environmental factors like climate change on skin health, teledermatology, applications of AI in clinical practice, and exploring integrative approaches to patient care. I am an active member of the Rheumatology-Dermatology Society, Medical Dermatology Society, and Society of Dermatology Hospitalists.
