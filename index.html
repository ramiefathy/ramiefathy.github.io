About

I am currently completing my final year of dermatology residency at Johns Hopkins University, with a focus on inflammatory and complex medical dermatology, dermatopathology, and the thoughtful application of generative AI in clinical workflows.

Incoming rheumatology-dermatology fellow at Mass General Brigham, joining Dr. Adam Rodman's research lab to study generative AI in medicine.

Princeton University graduate (Summa Cum Laude, Phi Beta Kappa) with molecular biology research experience in optogenetics and neural circuitry.

Leader in academic medicine, including service on the AAD Council on Science and Research (2025-2027) and Johns Hopkins House Staff Council.
