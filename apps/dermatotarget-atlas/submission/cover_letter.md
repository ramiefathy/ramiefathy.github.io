# Cover Letter Draft

Dear Editor,

We submit `DermatoTarget Atlas: A reproducible public-data target prioritization study across immune-mediated skin diseases` for consideration as an original investigation in translational computational dermatology.

This manuscript presents a fully reproducible public-data atlas integrating Open Targets, GWAS Catalog, Human Protein Atlas, ClinicalTrials.gov, and PubMed/Entrez evidence across six immune-mediated skin diseases. The study contributes an auditable target-disease score, validation analyses, pathway/module summaries, and submission-ready supplementary tables designed to support dermatology target selection and drug-repurposing hypothesis generation.

No protected health information, patient images, institutional chart data, or human-subjects intervention data were used. All source queries are public and cached with request provenance. The submitted materials include a reporting checklist, data/code availability statement, supplementary methods, and table manifest.

All authors should complete authorship, conflict-of-interest, funding, and ethics declarations before journal submission.
