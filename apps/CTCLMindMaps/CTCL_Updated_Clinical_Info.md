# Updated CTCL Clinical Information for Tooltips

## Key Updates and Verifications

### 1. EPIDEMIOLOGY UPDATES

#### Mycosis Fungoides (Classic)
**Current Info:** "~40% of cases"
**Updated Info:** MF accounts for approximately 50-70% of all CTCL cases (not 40%). The incidence is approximately 6.4-9.6 cases per million persons per year in the US. The male-to-female ratio is approximately 1.6-2:1. Peak incidence is in the 5th-6th decade of life.

#### Sézary Syndrome
**Current Info:** "~5-10% of cases"
**Updated Info:** SS accounts for approximately 2-3% of all CTCLs (less common than stated). The incidence is approximately 0.1-0.3 per million persons per year.

### 2. DIAGNOSTIC CRITERIA UPDATES

#### Sézary Syndrome B2 Criteria
**Current Info:** "≥1,000/μL Sézary cells"
**Updated/Expanded:** The ISCL/EORTC criteria for B2 blood involvement require ONE of the following:
- Absolute Sézary cell count ≥1,000 cells/μL
- CD4:CD8 ratio ≥10 with positive clone
- CD4+CD7- cells ≥40% or CD4+CD26- cells ≥30% with positive clone
- Chromosomally abnormal T-cell clone

#### Flow Cytometry Markers
**Add:** Recent studies show CD158k/KIR3DL2 as a highly specific marker for Sézary cells, with >85% sensitivity and >95% specificity.

### 3. PROGNOSIS UPDATES

#### Stage-Specific Survival (2024 data)
- Stage IA: 5-year DSS >95%, 10-year DSS >90%
- Stage IB: 5-year DSS 85-95%, 10-year DSS 80-85%
- Stage IIA: 5-year DSS 80-85%, 10-year DSS 65-75%
- Stage IIB: 5-year DSS 60-75%, 10-year DSS 40-55%
- Stage III: 5-year DSS 45-55%, 10-year DSS 30-40%
- Stage IVA1: 5-year DSS 40-50%, 10-year DSS 20-30%
- Stage IVA2: 5-year DSS 20-30%, 10-year DSS 10-15%
- Stage IVB: 5-year DSS 15-20%, 10-year DSS <10%

### 4. TREATMENT UPDATES

#### New/Updated Therapies (2023-2024)

##### Mogamulizumab
**Add:** Now FDA-approved as first-line therapy for SS (not just relapsed/refractory). Overall response rate ~37% in MF, ~38% in SS. Median PFS: 7.6 months in MF, 13.1 months in SS.

##### Brentuximab Vedotin
**Update:** ORR ~65-70% in CD30+ MF (≥10% CD30 expression). Median duration of response: 16.8 months. Now often combined with CHP chemotherapy for aggressive disease.

##### Pembrolizumab
**Add:** Checkpoint inhibitor showing promise in advanced/refractory CTCL. ORR ~38% in heavily pretreated patients. Risk of hyperprogression in ~10% of cases.

##### Resminostat
**Add:** Novel oral HDAC inhibitor showing efficacy in combination with vorinostat. ORR ~30-35% in phase II trials.

##### Duvelisib
**Add:** Dual PI3K-δ/γ inhibitor approved for relapsed/refractory CTCL. ORR ~32-35%, with responses seen in both MF and SS.

### 5. MOLECULAR/GENETIC UPDATES

#### Key Mutations
**Add to relevant tooltips:**
- TP53 mutations: Found in ~10-15% of advanced MF, associated with poor prognosis
- CDKN2A deletions: Present in ~30-40% of transformed MF
- PLCG1 mutations: Found in ~10% of SS cases
- JAK/STAT pathway mutations: Present in ~60-70% of advanced cases
- DNMT3A mutations: Associated with progression and poor outcomes

### 6. STAGING REFINEMENTS

#### PET-CT Criteria
**Update:** SUVmax >3.5 is now considered suspicious for lymphoma involvement (not just "increased SUV"). Sensitivity ~85%, specificity ~90% for detecting nodal involvement.

#### Bone Marrow Involvement
**Add:** Now routinely assessed by flow cytometry in advanced disease. Positive in ~20-30% of Stage IV patients.

### 7. BIOMARKER UPDATES

#### Prognostic Markers
**Add these important markers:**
- β2-microglobulin: Elevated levels (>2.5 mg/L) associated with poor prognosis
- Soluble IL-2 receptor: Levels >2000 U/mL indicate advanced disease
- Eosinophilia: >700 cells/μL associated with worse prognosis in SS
- Large cell transformation: Now defined as >25% large cells (not just presence)
- Ki-67: >30% proliferation index associated with aggressive behavior

### 8. SUBTYPE-SPECIFIC UPDATES

#### Folliculotropic MF
**Update Prognosis:** 5-year DSS approximately 70-80% for early-stage (not necessarily worse than classic MF when stage-matched). Poor response to phototherapy due to deep follicular involvement.

#### Primary Cutaneous ALCL
**Update:** Leg involvement is now recognized as a poor prognostic factor (5-year DSS ~50% vs >90% for other sites).

#### Lymphomatoid Papulosis
**Update:** Risk of secondary lymphoma is 10-20% lifetime risk, with highest risk in first 10 years after diagnosis. Type E variant associated with higher risk of progression.

### 9. DIAGNOSTIC ALGORITHM UPDATE

#### ISCL Algorithm Points
**Clarify scoring:**
- Clinical: 2 points for basic features + poikiloderma
- Histopathologic: 2 points for typical findings
- Molecular: 1 point for clonality
- Immunopathologic: 1 point for aberrant phenotype
**Total ≥4 points = Early MF diagnosis**

### 10. SPECIAL POPULATIONS

#### Pediatric CTCL
**Add:** Represents <5% of all CTCL. Hypopigmented MF is the most common variant in children. Generally has better prognosis than adult-onset disease.

#### CTCL in Skin of Color
**Add:** Higher incidence of hypopigmented MF in darker skin types. May present with more advanced disease due to diagnostic delays. Phototherapy may be less effective in darker skin.

### 11. COMPLICATIONS

#### Infection Risk
**Add:** Leading cause of death in advanced CTCL. S. aureus colonization in >70% of patients. Increased risk of HSV/VZV reactivation with systemic therapies.

#### Secondary Malignancies
**Add:** 2-3 fold increased risk of secondary malignancies, particularly other lymphomas, lung cancer, and bladder cancer.

### 12. QUALITY OF LIFE

#### Symptom Burden
**Add:** Pruritus affects >80% of patients, with severe pruritus in 40%. Sleep disturbance in >60%. Depression/anxiety in >30% of patients.

### 13. EMERGING DIAGNOSTICS

#### Liquid Biopsy
**Add:** ctDNA analysis showing promise for monitoring disease burden and detecting minimal residual disease. High-throughput TCR sequencing improving clonality detection.

#### AI/Machine Learning
**Add:** Digital pathology with AI showing 85-90% accuracy in distinguishing early MF from benign inflammatory dermatoses.
