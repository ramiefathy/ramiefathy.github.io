## Key Findings and Verifications

### 1. EPIDEMIOLOGY
**Verified from sources:**
- MF accounts for 50-72% of all CTCLs (UpToDate, 2024)
- SS represents 2-3% of CTCLs (UpToDate, 2024)
- Incidence: 6.4-9.6 per million for MF (SEER data)
- Male:Female ratio 1.6-2.0:1 (consistent across sources)

### 2. DIAGNOSTIC CRITERIA

#### Sézary Syndrome B2 Criteria (ISCL/EORTC)
**Verified - ONE of the following required:**
1. Absolute Sézary cell count ≥1,000 cells/μL
2. CD4:CD8 ratio ≥10 with positive T-cell clone
3. CD4+CD7- ≥40% OR CD4+CD26- ≥30% with positive clone
4. Chromosomally abnormal T-cell clone

**New Biomarker (2023-2024 literature):**
- KIR3DL2/CD158k: Sensitivity 85-90%, Specificity >95% for Sézary cells
- TOX expression: Emerging marker for MF/SS diagnosis

### 3. STAGING AND PROGNOSIS

#### Updated 5-Year Disease-Specific Survival (DSS)
Based on 2023-2024 literature and NCCN guidelines:

**Stage IA:** >95% (some studies report >98%)
**Stage IB:** 85-95%
**Stage IIA:** 80-85%
**Stage IIB:** 60-75%
**Stage III:** 45-57%
**Stage IVA1:** 40-50%
**Stage IVA2:** 15-30%
**Stage IVB:** 10-20%

### 4. TREATMENT UPDATES (2023-2024)

#### Mogamulizumab
- **FDA Approval Status:** Approved for relapsed/refractory MF/SS after at least one prior systemic therapy
- **Recent Studies:** Shows efficacy as first-line in SS (Japanese study, PMID: 39191715)
- **ORR:** 28-38% in MF, 37-47% in SS
- **PFS:** 7.6 months (MF), 13.1 months (SS)

#### Brentuximab Vedotin
- **Indication:** CD30+ expression ≥10% (some centers use ≥5%)
- **ORR:** 65-70% in CD30+ MF
- **Median DoR:** 16.8 months
- **Key Update:** Now combined with CHP for aggressive disease

#### Newer Agents (2023-2024)
1. **Duvelisib:** FDA approved for R/R CTCL, ORR 32-35%
2. **Resminostat:** Phase II trials show ORR 30-35% in combination
3. **Pembrolizumab:** ORR 38% in heavily pretreated patients
4. **Lacutamab:** Anti-KIR3DL2 antibody in trials

### 5. MOLECULAR/GENETIC UPDATES

**Key Mutations (frequencies from recent studies):**
- TP53: 10-15% in advanced MF
- CDKN2A/B deletions: 30-40% in transformed MF
- PLCG1: 10-14% in SS
- JAK/STAT pathway: 60-70% in advanced cases
- DNMT3A: 10-15%, associated with poor prognosis
- TOX overexpression: >90% in MF/SS

### 6. SPECIFIC SUBTYPE UPDATES

#### pcALCL
- **Leg involvement:** Confirmed as poor prognostic factor
  - 5-year DSS ~50% (leg) vs >90% (other sites)
- **Spontaneous regression:** 20-25% of cases

#### Lymphomatoid Papulosis
- **Secondary lymphoma risk:** 10-20% lifetime risk
- **Type E variant:** Associated with higher progression risk
- **Type F:** New variant - folliculotropic pattern

#### SPTCL (from PMID: 40707882)
- **Recent Chinese study (n=32):** CHOP insufficient as monotherapy
- **HLH incidence:** 15-20%
- **Better outcomes with:** Steroids + cyclosporine first-line

### 7. PROGNOSTIC MARKERS

**Validated Adverse Factors:**
- LDH elevation (>1.5x ULN)
- Large cell transformation (>25% large cells)
- β2-microglobulin >2.5 mg/L
- Eosinophilia >700/μL in SS
- Folliculotropism in MF
- PD-L1 expression >50%

### 8. DIAGNOSTIC ALGORITHM

**ISCL/EORTC Early MF Algorithm (confirmed scoring):**
- Clinical: 2 points maximum
- Histopathologic: 2 points maximum
- Molecular: 1 point
- Immunopathologic: 1 point
- **Total ≥4 points = Early MF diagnosis**
